This data must not be used as an operational, military, conclusive journalistic, financial, legal, or life-safety source. AI-Driven Aggregation

Data is aggregated and processed automatically by AI models. As such, it may contain hallucinations, misclassifications, duplications, source-related biases, erroneous geolocations, or unverified assessments.

Third-Party Sources & Fair Use

Impact Atlas aggregates metadata and provides links to publicly accessible third-party sources for purposes of research, commentary, analysis, and documentation. Original content remains the intellectual property of its respective owners. Whenever possible, the platform displays direct links to the original source rather than republishing third-party materials in their entirety.

Privacy & Data Protection

The project actively aims to avoid publishing personally identifiable information (PII). Any individual references present in the source material are anonymized, aggregated, or removed during processing. To report a potential exposure of personal data or an error, please use the "Report Error" button available within the event dossier or open an issue on GitHub.

Reports, corrections, and takedown requests can be submitted via GitHub Issues.
