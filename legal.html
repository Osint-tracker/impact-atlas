1. Nature of the Project

Impact Atlas is an experimental academic research framework designed for the study of Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) workflows, automated multi-source aggregation, and AI-driven geospatial analytics. This platform serves as a technical proof-of-concept for intelligence fusion and does not constitute a commercial or official service.

3. Military & Tactical Intelligence Constraints

Data visualized on this platform pertains to kinetic military events, strategic infrastructure, and tactical developments. Users must recognize that intelligence gathered from open sources is inherently subject to:

  • Deliberate disinformation or psychological operations (PsyOps) from various actors.
  • Operational Security (OPSEC) measures, including delayed or filtered reporting.
  • Technical limitations in geolocation and event attribution.
This system must NOT be utilized as a source for tactical decision-making, life-safety guidance, financial analysis, or official journalistic reporting.

4. AI-Driven Analytics & Limitations

The intelligence pipeline utilizes advanced AI models for automated extraction, normalization, and classification. Consequently, the output may contain:

  • Hallucinations: Generation of factually incorrect entities, numbers, or coordinates.
  • Misclassifications: Erroneous attribution of unit types, weapon systems, or event categories.
  • Source Bias: Propagation of political or tactical biases inherent in the original reporting.

5. Third-Party Content & Intellectual Property

Impact Atlas aggregates public metadata and provides direct links to third-party sources for research, documentation, and critical analysis under Fair Use principles. Original content remains the intellectual property of its respective owners. The platform prioritizes attribution and direct linking over full content republication to respect the integrity of external sources.

6. Privacy & Data Protection Protocols

The project adheres to strict protocols to prevent the publication of Personally Identifiable Information (PII). Any personal identifiers present in source materials are anonymized, aggregated, or redacted during processing. To report a potential exposure of sensitive data or a technical error, users are encouraged to utilize the "Report Error" function within the event dossier or open a formal request via GitHub.
